Logo options

Bear + play, four ways. Each shown large, at taskbar sizes (16 / 24 / 32 px), and in one colour on light.

A · Cub

Designer's pick

Solid bear head, play triangle cut out as the face. Simplest and clearest at 16 px.

B · Den

An app-icon tile with the bear cut out. Bold in the taskbar, but the play shrinks to a speck at 16 px.

C · Line

One continuous outline around a solid play. Matches Windows 11's line icons, but looks light next to solid ones.

D · Snout

A round play button becomes the muzzle. Most character, but the play disappears at 16 px.
