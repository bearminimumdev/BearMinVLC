pill

Rows 6 · buttons 10 · menus 14 · dialogs 20 · actions are pills. Buttons are 44 px; icons 24 px at a 1.5 px stroke. Sliders 4 px (seek grows to 6 px on hover). One 20 px edge everywhere. Window buttons follow Windows 11: square, 46 × 32, red on close hover.

Behaviour

Controls fade after 2.5 s still (200 ms), cursor too; 0.5 s after the pointer leaves. They stay while paused, a menu is open, the pointer is on them, or at end / error / loading.

Keys don't wake the controls — they show the small top pill for 1 s instead. Tab does bring the controls back.

Mouse: click video = pause (brief centre flash) · double-click = fullscreen · wheel = volume ±5% (seek ±5 s over the bar) · drag = move window · right-click = more.

Resume automatically with a 5 s “Start over” pill. Positions save between 30 s and 95%, with the chosen subtitle and audio tracks.

One window: “Open with” replaces the current video. Dropping a subtitle adds it; dropping a video replaces it.

Errors and loading show in the window, never as pop-ups. A spinner appears only after 0.4 s. The screen stays awake while playing; media keys work.
